Why shops switch to Khatiwada POS

The same shop, run a little differently.

With notebooks or a basic register

Staff have to ask you, or just guess, the price of every item, slowing down each sale.

Billing is handwritten or entered one line at a time, slow, and easy to get wrong.

Stock is tracked by memory or on paper, items go missing without anyone noticing.

Old sales are hard to look back on, there's no simple way to check last month's numbers.

Usually only the owner really understands how everything is organized.

A real POS system usually means an expensive machine and a technician to set it up.

With Khatiwada POS

Scan the barcode or QR code and the price shows up by itself, any staff member can ring up a sale.

Each item's price and the running total add up automatically as you go, no manual entry line by line.

Stock updates automatically with every sale, so you always know what's left.

Every sale is recorded automatically, check any day's or month's numbers anytime.

Any staff member can use it comfortably, in English or Nepali.

Runs on the phone you already own, no dedicated hardware, no technician needed.
